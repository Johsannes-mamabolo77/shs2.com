About Us

Our Mission

Sebenza Hiring Solution is dedicated to bridging the gap between skilled domestic workers and families or businesses in need of their services. We believe in creating meaningful employment opportunities while providing reliable staffing solutions.

Founded in 2015, we've successfully placed over 1,000 professionals in homes and businesses across the region, helping to reduce unemployment while improving the quality of domestic services.

Our team of experienced recruiters carefully vets all candidates to ensure they meet our high standards of professionalism and skill.

Our Services

For Job Seekers

  • Job placement in housekeeping, nanny services, and house maintenance
  • Resume building assistance and interview preparation
  • Guidance on career advancement within the domestic industry
  • Skills development workshops
  • Ongoing career support and counseling

For Employers

  • Finding qualified domestic workers (housekeepers, nannies, maintenance staff)
  • Background checks and reference verification for all candidates
  • Tailored staffing solutions based on employer needs
  • Temporary and permanent placement options
  • Replacement guarantee for unsatisfactory placements

Our Process

  1. Initial consultation to understand your needs
  2. Careful screening and matching process
  3. Interview coordination
  4. Placement and follow-up
  5. Ongoing support and mediation if needed

Find Qualified Staff
